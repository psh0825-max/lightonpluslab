Open lens design files in the browser

Open CODE V .seq and Zemax OpticStudio .zmx files as they are and read ray traces, spot diagrams, MTF and aberrations. The file is computed inside your browser and never sent to a server. A lens prescription is the design, so it could not work any other way.

Lens dataSurfaces, radii, thickness, glass and index — read like a prescription sheet.
Aberration and distortionAstigmatic field curves (T and S) next to distortion against f·tanθ and f·θ.
Through-focus MTFResponse by frequency across a focus shift of ±5 to 50 µm.
Zoom positionsSwitch between zoom positions. A sample lens is included to open right away.

Why recording and summarising a lecture still does not make it stick

Transcription and summaries are everywhere now. The paradox is that the tidier the note, the less you go back to it. On the forgetting curve and what spaced repetition fills in.

Why the lyrics drifted up to 16 seconds — match, do not count

Karaoke lyrics drifted further the longer the song ran, and worst in Korean. The culprit was not the timestamps but how we counted words. Replacing proportional estimation with character-offset matching.
