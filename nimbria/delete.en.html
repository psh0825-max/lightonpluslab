How to delete your Nimbria account and data

A Nimbria (LightOn Plus Lab (라이트온 플러스 랩)) account can be deleted at any time. There are two ways — delete it yourself in the app, or ask by e-mail.

1. Yourself, in the app (the fastest way)

  1. Open the app.
  2. Open Settings (the gear).
  3. In the Info tab, tap Delete account.
  4. In the confirmation window, tap Delete.

The moment you tap, the account row on the server is deleted, and after the server answers that it has deleted it, the account number, session key, nickname, progress and consent record kept on the device are deleted too (if the server is not deleted first, the device is not cleared either — so that you can tap again). When you open the app again you are a guest starting for the first time, beginning again from the consent on the first screen. The settings (controls, display, sound) and the downloaded game files have nothing to do with the account, so they remain on the device as they are.

If you linked the account to a Google account, that link disappears as well — even if you sign in again with the same Google account, the deleted account does not come back (you can start again from scratch with the same Google account). We also ask Android to remove the trace it keeps that "this Google account was used with this app".

It cannot be deleted while the internet is disconnected — the account row on the server has to be deleted together, so please tap again after connecting. If the device has never connected to online matches, there is no account to delete on the server, so only the data kept on the device is deleted.

While your use is restricted, the app does not connect to the server, so this button cannot be used — please ask by e-mail as in section 2 below (Terms of Service, Article 9(5)).

2. By e-mail (or through the in-app contact form)

Write to support@lightonpluslab.com with the following. You can also send it through the in-app contact form (Settings → Info → Contact · Report a bug — category "account or records"). The form sends the short account number along automatically, so just write the e-mail address at which you want to receive the result.

  • Subject: Account deletion request (or in Korean: 계정 삭제 요청)
  • Nickname (exactly the name you used in the game)
  • The account number, if one is shown in Settings → Info in the app (if it is not shown, you need not write it)
  • Roughly the date you last played (used to find the account when several overlap)

As this Service does not receive sign-up information (e-mail address, phone number), the only clues for checking that the person asking is the owner of the account are the nickname and the account number. So deleting it yourself in the app is surer and faster. Requests received by e-mail are handled within 10 days of receipt, and we tell you the result.

It cannot be undone. The Operator cannot restore it either.

4. What remains, and why

  • One row per match remains — when, on which map, which hero in which position produced which result, and the seed with which that match can be replayed. That match also contains the records of the other nine people, so it cannot be deleted as a whole, and it is also where the cause is looked for when a bug occurs. However, the account number that points to you is erased from that row, so the remaining row is not linked to any account. This row too is deleted after 1 year.
  • The input records inside a match (for replaying) contain neither an account number nor a nickname in the first place. Only the latest 200 matches are kept; beyond that they disappear.
  • The server log records neither nicknames nor IP addresses. The rows that remain say about as much as "connection number so-and-so did what, when", and they disappear after at most 30 days.
  • Error reports sent from the device contain neither a nickname nor an account number nor a device number (only which version and which line broke). Regardless of deletion, they disappear after 30 days.
  • If the account had received a restriction, the reason code and the end time of that restriction remain (the memo written by the Operator is deleted). This is to prevent escaping a restriction by deleting the account and coming back; for a restriction with no time limit, that row too is deleted 1 year after the day of deletion.
  • Records of you reporting other people remain because they concern the reported person, but your account number is erased, so it can no longer be known who made them. They disappear 90 days after the day they were received.
  • Inquiries sent without an account number (sent from a device that has never connected to online matches) are not linked to an account and so are not deleted automatically. If you want them deleted, tell us by e-mail. Even otherwise they disappear 1 year after the day they were received.
  • If there is data that a law or regulation requires to be kept longer, it is kept for that period. At present the Service has no payments, so there is no such data.

5. What happens if I only delete the app?

If you delete the app, the account number kept on the device disappears, so you cannot go back to that account. However, the account row on the server stays as it is and is deleted automatically once 2 years after the last connection have passed. If you want it deleted right now, do section 1 above before deleting the app, or tell us by e-mail as in section 2.

It is different if you linked the account to a Google account — if you delete the app, install it again and sign in with the same Google account, you can come back to that account ("Restore My Account" on the first screen). So "only deleting the app" is not deleting the account. If you want the account gone, please request deletion through section 1 or section 2 above.
