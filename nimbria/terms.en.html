Nimbria Terms of Service

Article 1 (Purpose)

These terms set out what is needed to use the mobile game Nimbria (the "Service") provided by LightOn Plus Lab (라이트온 플러스 랩) (the "Operator") — what the Operator and the user each do and do not do.

Article 2 (Definitions)

  1. Service — the game the Operator provides under the name Nimbria and everything that comes with it.
  2. User — a person who uses the Service.
  3. Guest account — an account that continues through a number made on the device, with no sign-up step. At present this is the only kind of account in the Service.
  4. Online match — the feature of connecting to the server and playing 5v5 against other users.
  5. Solo play — AI matches are computed on the device. If the device has a server account, a summary of each AI match is synced to restore level and goals across devices. Match records on a device that never connects to the server stay local.
  6. Report — a user telling the Operator, on the result screen of an online match, about another user of the same match with one of the fixed reasons (Article 9(6)).

Article 3 (Posting and changing the terms)

  1. The Operator keeps these terms inside the Service (Settings → Info → Terms of Service) and at an address anyone can open, lightonpluslab.com/nimbria/terms.html. The full text of the terms is included in the app, so it can be read without the internet.
  2. The first time the app is opened, the user must agree separately to these terms and to the Privacy Policy in order to use the Service. The version number and time of your agreement are recorded on the device and can be seen in Settings → Info.
  3. The Operator may change the terms when necessary. When it does, it states the version number and effective date and gives notice through the in-Service announcements and this page at least 7 days before the effective date (at least 30 days before for changes unfavourable to users). When the version number goes up, the app shows the changed text again and asks for agreement again.
  4. If you do not agree to the changed terms, you may stop using the Service (deleting the account — Article 6). If you keep using the Service after the notice period has passed, you are deemed to have agreed to the changed terms.

Article 4 (Content of the Service and charges)

  1. Downloading the Service and playing matches costs no money (basic use is free).
  2. As of the effective date of this version, the Service has no paid products, no probability-based items and no advertising. The Operator may sell paid products or carry advertising in the future. In that case it gives notice beforehand, before introducing them, in the manner of Article 3(3), and Articles 11 and 12 apply to paid products. Before advertising is carried, the Privacy Policy is changed to tell you about the newly used data.
  3. Gold, items and traits in the game are game elements used only within one match. They are not bought with money and have no cash value.
  4. Telecommunication charges for using the Service are paid by the user under the user's contract with their carrier.
  5. The Operator may change the content of the Service (heroes, map, items, rules, how rank is worked out and so on) in order to improve it. Changes are applied through the update received when the game starts.

Article 5 (Eligibility — 14 or over)

  1. Only people aged 14 or over may use the Service. The first time the app is opened, we ask the year and month of your birth to check that you are 14 or over; that value is used only to work out your age and is not stored. As we do not ask the day of your birth, the month of birth is counted as fully passed (in the month that contains your 14th birthday you pass only from the following month).
  2. If you are found to be under 14, you are told on the spot that you cannot use the Service and you are not asked again for 24 hours. If it is later confirmed that a user is under 14, the Operator deletes that account and its data.

Article 6 (Guest account — tied to the device, and linkable if you wish)

  1. There is no sign-up step. The first time the app is opened, the device makes a single random number, and the server hashes it irreversibly and uses it as the account number. There is no e-mail address and no password.
  2. So the account is by default tied to that device. You must know the following (this is the case where you have not done the account linking of paragraph 6).
    • If you delete the app, the number kept on the device disappears with it. If you install it again, you become a guest starting for the first time, and you cannot go back to the earlier nickname, rank, match record and progress.
    • The same applies if you clear the device's storage (deleting app data) or move to another device. The app does not back up the account to the cloud (if it did, the same account would come alive on two devices).
    • The Operator cannot restore an account that has disappeared. As there is no sign-up information, there is no way to confirm that the account was yours.
  3. One account can be connected from only one place at a time. If the same account connects again, the earlier connection is dropped.
  4. Account deletion — the user may delete the account at any time (Settings → Info → Delete account, or support@lightonpluslab.com). When it is deleted, the nickname, rank, match record and progress disappear irreversibly. After the server answers that it has deleted them, the account number, session key, nickname and progress kept on the device are deleted too, while the settings and the downloaded game files remain as they are. While your use is restricted you cannot use the button in the app, so please ask by e-mail (Article 9(5)). Details are in the Account deletion guide and the Privacy Policy.
  5. Accounts not used for a long time — if there is no connection for 2 years (730 days) after the last connection, the account and its records are deleted automatically (Privacy Policy, section 4).
  6. Account linking — the user may link the guest account to a Google account (Settings → Info → "Link account"). It is optional: every feature of the game can be used just the same without it.
    • Once linked, you can come back to that account after deleting the app or changing devices by signing in with the same Google account. "Restore My Account", shown the first time the app is opened, is the way to do so.
    • The only thing the Operator receives and stores is one account number that Google gives, and even that is hashed irreversibly. The e-mail address and name that come along in the sign-in ID token are not stored (Privacy Policy, sections 1 and 6).
    • One Google account per account, and one account per device. An account already linked to another Google account cannot be linked again, and if another account is linked to the Google account you are trying to load, the Operator shows the two accounts side by side and lets the user choose. If you do not choose, neither disappears.
    • If you choose "Load that account", the account used on this device is deleted — when something will be deleted, we write down what will disappear and ask for confirmation once more before deleting. A deleted account cannot be restored.
    • The way to undo the link is account deletion (paragraph 4). When the account is deleted the link disappears too, and you can start again from scratch with the same Google account.
    • A restricted account cannot be loaded (Article 9(5)). A restriction attaches to the account, so account linking cannot be used to escape it.
    • This feature is visible only when the Operator has turned it on (it can be used only in the Android app and does not appear in a web browser). For the iPhone version, a way of linking with an Apple account will be provided along with it.

Article 7 (Nickname)

  1. A nickname is 2-12 characters long, and only Hangul (complete syllables), Latin letters, digits and the underscore (_) can be used. It cannot contain spaces, emoji or invisible characters, or consist only of Hangul consonants or vowels (ㅋㅋㅋ) or only of digits.
  2. No two nicknames may be the same. Names are treated as the same regardless of upper or lower case and of character form (Kara and kara are the same name). If a name is already in use, the server suggests a few names that can be used.
  3. The following nicknames are refused — profanity and hate speech, and words impersonating the staff or an official account (운영자 (operator), 관리자 (administrator), 고객센터 (customer centre), 공식 (official), admin, GM, support, nimbria and the like).
  4. The nickname can be changed inside the Service. The Operator may take back a nickname that breaks paragraph 3 or that confuses other users, and have it chosen again.

Article 8 (What users must not do)

  1. Altering or imitating the game program or its communication (abnormal clients, macros, automated play, memory tampering, forging patch files).
  2. Putting excessive load on the server (repeating requests beyond the allowed share, opening many connections from one address, creating accounts in bulk with automated tools).
  3. Harassing other users — impersonating nicknames, losing on purpose (trolling), and leaving matches on purpose again and again (leaving a match — Article 9(3)).
  4. Exploiting a bug or loophole of the Service without telling us. If you find one, please tell us (Settings → Info → Contact · Report a bug) — that helps the Service most.
  5. Acts that break laws or regulations or infringe another person's rights.
  6. False reports — reporting something you know did not happen, or reporting repeatedly to harass the same person (Article 9(6)).

Article 9 (When the terms are broken — what the server actually does today)

The measures the Operator can take are as follows. Paragraphs 1-6 below are all in the server today (paragraph 5 was added on 2026-11-14, paragraph 6 on 2026-11-16).

  1. Blocking connections that exceed their share — there are limits on the number of messages, bytes and commands one connection may send per second. Messages over the limit are dropped, and if the limit is exceeded by far (20 times in a row) the connection is cut. Sending malformed messages 20 times also cuts it. There is also a limit on the number of connections that can be opened from one address.
  2. Clearing silent connections — if there is no message for 10 seconds, the connection is considered dropped and is closed. If nothing happens for 10 minutes outside a match, the connection is closed. (Neither affects the account.)
  3. Not playing a match to the end (abandoning the match — "leaving a match") — if you leave during a match or your connection drops, the AI takes over that hero, and the position stays that account's until the match ends (you can come back at any time). If, when the match ends, it is judged as leaving a match, rank points are deducted — even if your team won they do not go up, and they go down by at least 20 points. However, rank points never fall below 0, so if fewer points remain they become 0, and if they are already 0 they stay there. Experience, mastery and contribution remain as fought for. If you come back and play the match to the end, it is not leaving a match. If it is judged as leaving a match, the app tells you once, at your next connection (or on the spot), which match it was and how much the points changed.
  4. Refusing and taking back nicknames — Article 7(3).
  5. Restriction — where a user breaks Article 8 seriously or repeatedly and harms other users' use, the Operator may restrict that account's use of online matches. What actually happens is this.
  • Only online matches are blocked. A restricted account cannot connect to the server and cannot join the queue. If it is connected at the moment of restriction, the connection is cut on the spot, and if it was in a match, the AI takes over that hero so that the match of the remaining people runs to the end. Solo play can be used as before.
    • The reason and the period are shown in the app. When a restricted account connects, a screen appears showing the reason, the end time (in the device's time), the memo written by the Operator, and the e-mail address for objections.
    • It is lifted automatically when the period is over. A restriction can also be imposed without a time limit, in which case the screen shows "No time limit".
    • You can object — write to support@lightonpluslab.com with the account number (shown on the restriction notice screen). The Operator will look at it again and reverse the measure if it was wrong.
    • The right to delete the account remains during a restriction. However, as the app does not connect to the server, the "Delete account" button in the app cannot be used — if you ask at the e-mail address above, we delete it within 10 days. Even if the account is deleted, the reason code and the end time of that restriction remain (to prevent escaping a restriction by deleting and coming back; the memo written by the Operator is deleted — Privacy Policy, section 4).
    • Where the breach of Article 8 is very serious (for example an attack that brings the Service down), the Operator may also delete the account. In that case too the reason is given by e-mail.
  • Report — if you think another user has broken Article 8, you can report a user who was in that match with you on the result screen of the online match (the AI and yourself cannot be reported). What actually happens is this.
  • The reason is chosen from five fixed ones — left during the match and did not come back · was present but did nothing · died on purpose or ruined the match · seems to have used an abnormal program or a bug · the nickname is profanity or hate speech, or impersonates someone. No text is written. The evidence is the record of that match held by the Operator.
    • You can report the same user once per match and up to ten times per day (24 hours), and reports are accepted only within one day after the match ends. When it is accepted, the app tells you "Received".
    • A report alone leads to no measure. The server only counts reports and shows them to the Operator; the restriction of paragraph 5 is imposed only when the Operator looks at the record of that match in person and judges that Article 8 was broken. No one is restricted merely because there are many reports.
    • The reported user is told neither that a report was made nor who made it. The result of the Operator's review is not separately told to the reporting user (a user who receives a restriction is told the reason and the period, as in paragraph 5).
    • A false report is in itself a breach of Article 8 (Article 8(6)). The Operator can look separately at the reports made by one account, and for reports made falsely on purpose or repeated in order to harass, it may impose the restriction of paragraph 5 on the reporting account.
    • What a report contains and how long it is kept (90 days) are in the Privacy Policy, sections 1 and 4.

If you are away for more than 25% of a match, your visible rank points cannot increase for that match, even if you return before it ends. Rank losses from a defeat still apply. A short disconnection followed by a return receives normal rewards if it does not exceed this threshold.
