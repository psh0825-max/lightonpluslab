Why Korean doesn't sound the way it's spelled

You can read Hangul now. Then you hear someone say 감사합니다 and it doesn't match the letters at all.

That isn't your ear. Korean spelling shows what a word is made of, and the sounds shift when the letters meet. There are only a handful of rules behind nearly all of it. Here are the nine you'll run into most, with the spelling on the left and what people actually say on the right.

1. Linking: the last consonant moves over

When a syllable ends in a consonant and the next one starts with a vowel, the consonant slides across. This is the one you'll hear in almost every sentence.

WrittenSaidMeaning
한국어한구거Korean language
맛있어요마시써요it's delicious
이름이 뭐예요?이르미 뭐예요?what's your name?

2. Nasalization: stops turn into nasals

A ㄱ, ㄷ or ㅂ sound right before ㄴ or ㅁ becomes ㅇ, ㄴ or ㅁ. It's why the most common phrase in the language catches everyone out.

WrittenSaidMeaning
감사합니다감사함니다thank you
국물궁물broth
학년항년school year
첫눈천눈first snow

3. Tense consonants

After a ㄱ, ㄷ or ㅂ sound, the next plain consonant tightens. It never shows in the spelling, but you hear it constantly.

WrittenSaidMeaning
학교학꾜school
택시택씨taxi
국밥국빱rice soup

4. Aspiration: ㅎ merges with its neighbor

ㅎ next to ㄱ, ㄷ, ㅂ or ㅈ combines with it into one breathy sound: ㅋ, ㅌ, ㅍ or ㅊ.

WrittenSaidMeaning
축하추카congratulations
입학이팍starting school
한국어 잘 못해요한구거 잘 모태요my Korean isn't good

5. Silent ㅎ

A final ㅎ disappears before a vowel. If there was another consonant with it, that one links over.

WrittenSaidMeaning
좋아조아I like it
싫어시러I don't like it
많아마나there's a lot

6. Palatalization: 같이 isn't "gat-i"

Before the vowel 이, ㅌ turns into ㅊ and ㄷ turns into ㅈ.

WrittenSaidMeaning
같이가치together
해돋이해도지sunrise
굳이구지needlessly

7. ㄴ next to ㄹ becomes ㄹ

The two can't sit side by side as they are, so the ㄴ gives way.

WrittenSaidMeaning
신라실라Silla, the old kingdom
연락열락contact
설날설랄Lunar New Year

8. ㄹ becomes ㄴ

After ㅁ or ㅇ, ㄹ is said as ㄴ. After ㄱ both letters change at once, which is why 국립 looks nothing like its sound.

WrittenSaidMeaning
심리심니psychology
담력담녁courage
국립궁닙national

9. Only seven sounds at the end of a syllable

There are many final consonant spellings, but a syllable can only end in seven sounds: ㄱ, ㄴ, ㄷ, ㄹ, ㅁ, ㅂ, ㅇ. So different spellings collapse into the same ending. Double finals work the same way, and you only say one of the two letters.

WrittenSaidMeaning
옷옫clothes
꽃꼳flower
부엌부억kitchen
닭닥chicken
값갑price

You don't have to memorize this

Nobody thinks "nasalization" mid-sentence. Knowing the rules just tells you what to listen for. After that it's mostly hearing the words enough times, so say them out loud and copy a native speaker rather than reading the charts again.
