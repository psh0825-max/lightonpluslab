English

SCORE STUDIO ("the app") is a score editing and arranging app by LightOn Plus Lab.

The app is fully offline by default. It transmits no data and operates no servers.

Score image recognition (optional): Only when you explicitly select the OpenAI Vision backend in settings and enter your own API key, the single image you submit for recognition is sent to OpenAI (US). Transmission occurs only upon your request, and the developer has no access to the image. OpenAI's handling is governed by the OpenAI Privacy Policy.

API keys are stored only on your device, are never sent to the developer, and can be deleted in settings at any time.

We do not collect scores, edits, or any personal information, and we use no ads or analytics.
